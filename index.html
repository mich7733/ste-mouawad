A Legacy of Faith & Artistry

A Family Legacy in Stone

Since 1967, STE. MOUAWAD has specialized in churches, altars, sculptures, fireplaces, and custom marble creations throughout Lebanon and beyond.

Every project combines artistic vision, technical expertise, and generations of craftsmanship.

A family business founded in 1967 and still family-run today, working from our workshop in Mazraat Yachouh, Mount Lebanon.

Ste. Mouawad

What We Make

We hand-craft all kinds of stonework in natural stone and marble. Every piece is designed, drawn and hand-finished in our Lebanese workshop.

Church Interiors

Sanctuaries, niches and complete interior stonework for new churches and restorations.

Altars & Devotional Pieces

Carved altars, tabernacles and devotional elements in marble and stone.

Fireplaces

From classic carved mantels to minimalist modern surrounds, made to measure for homes and villas.

Columns & Balusters

Fluted columns, capitals and hand-turned balusters for interiors and facades.

Sculpture & Carved Details

Medallions, crosses, reliefs and one-of-a-kind sculptural pieces.

Gardens & Outdoor

Stone fountains, outdoor barbecues and garden features built to last.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Ste. Mouawad make?

+

Custom work in natural stone and marble: church interiors and altars, fireplaces, columns and balusters, fountains and outdoor pieces, and hand-carved sculptural details. In short, all kinds of handcrafted stonework, each piece designed and made to order.

Where is Ste. Mouawad based?

+

The family workshop is in Mazraat Yachouh, in the Metn district of Mount Lebanon. The company was founded there in 1967.

Do you work outside Lebanon?

+

Yes. We take on projects across Lebanon and beyond. Message us on WhatsApp to discuss your location and project.

How does a custom stone project work?

+

Every commission follows five steps: design, technical drawings, stone selection, hand craftsmanship in our workshop, and installation on site.

Who do you work with?

+

Churches and parishes, homeowners, architects, interior designers and contractors.

How do I request a quote?

+

Message us on WhatsApp at +961 3 269 710 with photos, measurements or a sketch of what you have in mind. You can also reach us on Instagram (@ste.mouawad) or Facebook.
